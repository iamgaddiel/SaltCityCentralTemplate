About SaltCity Central

SaltCity Central is the headquarters of SaltCity Ministry, located in the heart of Warri. We are a multi-generational community where families, singles, and believers of all ages gather for worship, discipleship, and fellowship.

As the flagship expression, we host our major conferences, leadership training programs, and serve as the spiritual home for hundreds of believers committed to growing in faith and fulfilling their ministry.

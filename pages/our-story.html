Our Journey of Faith

SaltCity Central began with a simple but profound vision: to create a community where believers could be discipled to fulfill their God-given ministry. What started as a small gathering has grown into a vibrant movement across Warri and Delta State.

In 2018, Pastor Tobore David and a small group of committed believers began meeting regularly for prayer, Bible study, and fellowship. They shared a common burden to see believers equipped and empowered to live out their faith authentically and impact their world for Christ.

The name "SaltCity" reflects our dual calling: to be salt that preserves and flavors our world with the gospel, and to build a city—a community—where God's people can grow, serve, and fulfill their purpose together.

Our Journey

Timeline of Growth

Key moments in our story

2018

The Beginning

SaltCity Central was birthed from a burden to disciple believers beyond Sunday services. Initial meetings began with a small group committed to prayer and Bible study.

2019

First Sunday Service

Our first official Sunday service was held with a growing congregation. The vision for multiple expressions across Warri began to take shape.

2020

Launch of Discipleship Groups

We introduced Company Groups—small discipleship communities that meet weekly for Bible study, prayer, and mutual encouragement.

2021

Expansion to Campuses

Cityzens expressions were launched at PTI and FUPRE, bringing the gospel and discipleship to university campuses.

2022

Centre of Discipleship

We secured and began developing our permanent facility at 20 Okumagba Avenue, establishing the Centre of Discipleship.

2023

Multiple Expressions

SaltCity grew to include CityCenter (young adults), Life City, and our Sapele expression, reaching diverse communities across Delta State.

Today

Continuing the Mission

We continue to disciple believers, plant new expressions, and equip God's people to fulfill their ministry in every sphere of society.

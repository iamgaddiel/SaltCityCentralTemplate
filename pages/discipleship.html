Our Discipleship System

At SaltCity Central, our discipleship classes are designed as a progressive journey to help you grow in faith and fulfill your ministry calling. Under the leadership of our Headmistress, Deacon Nancy, we offer a flexible, rolling enrollment system where you can start at any time from the beginning. Multiple teachers are available during class sessions to provide personalized guidance and support.

Headmistress
Deacon Nancy

Our core program follows a clear progression: Begin with the School of Discipleship for foundational teaching, advance to the School of Membership for church integration, and complete with the School of Maturity for deeper growth. Upon completion, explore our specialized faculty courses for targeted development in specific life areas.
